Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version: we don't collect personal data. Everything you type into Hovsa stays on your phone.

Hovsa is a party-games app made by Mathias Erfurt Nielsen, an independent developer in Denmark ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

What stays on your phone

The player names you enter, the games and settings you choose, which cards you've already seen, and whether you've unlocked Premium. They're saved only on your device so the app remembers them between games. We never receive them, and deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No account or login.
  • No ads, no tracking, and nothing that watches how you play.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • The app doesn't ask for your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Buying Premium

Hovsa Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card details. To check purchases and let you restore them on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). When you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives the store receipt and a random ID created by the app, not your name or email address. RevenueCat also shows us sales figures, such as how many people have bought Premium. RevenueCat's handling is covered by its privacy policy, and Apple's and Google's by theirs.

Sharing results

If you share a Party Report picture, it's made on your phone and sent through your phone's own share menu to the app you pick. We don't receive it. Keep in mind that the picture shows the player names from your game.

Children

Hovsa is made for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data about you. Because we don't store personal data, there's usually nothing for us to hand over, but you're always welcome to contact us. For purchase records, contact Apple, Google or RevenueCat. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

This website

This website has no cookies or analytics. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Hovsa ever starts collecting data, for example crash reports, we'll say so here first.
