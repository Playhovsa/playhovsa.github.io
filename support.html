Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea for a card? Write to hovsa.apps@gmail.com. We read everything and usually reply within a few days.

I bought Premium, but it's still locked

Open the settings in Hovsa and tap Restore purchase. Make sure your phone is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play through Google Play Help.

Does Hovsa need the internet?

No. Every game works offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore Premium.

Where are our player names saved?

Only on your phone. The privacy policy has the details.

A card is offensive or wrong

Sorry about that! Email us the text on the card and we'll fix it in the next update.

Can I suggest cards or a new game?

Yes, please. The best ones come from real game nights.
